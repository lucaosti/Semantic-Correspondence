\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Benchmarks.}
PF-Willow and PF-Pascal~\cite{ham2016proposal,ham2017proposal} were the first keypoint benchmarks for semantic correspondence, but their pairs show limited changes of pose and scale. SPair-71k~\cite{min2019spair} provides 70{,}958 pairs over 18 categories with annotated viewpoint, scale, truncation and occlusion difficulty (\cref{fig:teaser}), and is the standard benchmark today. Accuracy is measured with the Percentage of Correct Keypoints (PCK): a prediction is correct if it lies within $\alpha\cdot\max(h,w)$ of the ground truth, where $(h,w)$ is the target bounding box.

\paragraph{Foundation features for correspondence.}
Self-supervised ViTs such as DINO~\cite{caron2021emerging} and DINOv2~\cite{oquab2023dinov2} produce patch features in which object parts cluster across instances; DINOv3~\cite{simeoni2025dinov3} scales the recipe and adds a Gram-anchoring loss to keep dense features sharp. SAM~\cite{kirillov2023segment} is trained for promptable, class-agnostic segmentation, so its encoder is a natural test of whether boundary-aware features also carry part semantics. Diffusion features are an alternative~\cite{tang2023emergent}: Zhang \etal~\cite{zhang2023tale} show that Stable Diffusion and DINOv2 are complementary and that their fusion is a strong zero-shot matcher.

\paragraph{Supervised refinement.}
With keypoint supervision, a light head on frozen features~\cite{zhang2023tale}, learned prompts for a diffusion model~\cite{li2023sd4match}, or a geometry-aware adapter~\cite{zhang2024telling} improve on zero-shot matching by a large margin. GeoAware-SC~\cite{zhang2024telling} also replaces the argmax by a \emph{window soft-argmax} at inference, which we adopt. Instead of adding a head, we adapt the backbone itself, either directly or through Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA)~\cite{hu2022lora}, a parameter-efficient alternative to adapters~\cite{houlsby2019parameter,chen2022adaptformer}.

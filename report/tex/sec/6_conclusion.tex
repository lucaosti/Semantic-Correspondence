\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We compared DINOv2, DINOv3 and SAM as dense feature extractors for semantic correspondence on SPair-71k, from training-free matching to light supervised adaptation. Frozen last-block features of DINOv2 give $53.2$ PCK@0.1, in agreement with published and independent results. Fine-tuning its last two blocks for what amounts to a single epoch, and refining the argmax with a window soft-argmax, raises this to $84.9$ ($71.7$ at $\alpha{=}0.05$), on par with a state-of-the-art method built on frozen SD+DINOv2 features. LoRA on four blocks reaches over $90\%$ of the gain of fine-tuning the same blocks with two orders of magnitude fewer trainable parameters, and WSA improves every adapted model at no training cost. SAM's segmentation features remain far behind, which shows that boundary awareness alone does not provide part-level semantics. Higher input resolution, checkpoint selection on validation PCK and explicit handling of symmetric parts are the most promising next steps.
